Cake Recipe

Ingredients:
1½ cups all-purpose flour
1 cup sugar
½ cup butter (softened)
2 eggs
1½ tsp baking powder
½ cup milk
1 tsp vanilla essence
A pinch of salt

Instructions:
1. Prepare the Batter
In a bowl, mix butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
Add eggs one by one, beating well after each addition.
Add vanilla essence.

2. Dry Ingredients
In another bowl, mix:
Flour
Baking powder
Salt
Add this dry mixture slowly into the wet mixture.

3. Add Milk
Pour in the milk and mix gently until the batter is smooth (don’t overmix).

4. Bake
Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
Grease a cake tin and pour the batter.
Bake for 30–35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.

5. Cool and Serve
Let the cake cool, slice it, and enjoy your soft and fluffy vanilla butter cake!
